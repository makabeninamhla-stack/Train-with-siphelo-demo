FAQ

Questions before you start?

Can I join before paying?

Yes. You first create your account and choose your goal. Payment only happens when you select a membership plan.

Is this a once-off payment?

No. The membership is a recurring monthly subscription that continues until cancelled.

Will my workouts match my goal?

Yes. Your chosen goal personalises the workouts and community you see in the app.

Can Siphelo communicate with members?

Yes. Coach Siphelo can post tips, messages, workouts and motivation inside each goal community.
